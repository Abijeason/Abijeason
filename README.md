<td valign="top">

**A free gym workout guide that tells you exactly what to do today.**

- Builds a weekly plan around your goal, experience and the equipment your gym has
- Guides each workout set by set, with suggested weights and a rest timer
- Friends share one plan, and each phone sets its own sets, reps and weights
- Works offline, needs no account and keeps workout history on the phone

`React` `TypeScript` `Vite` `Vitest` `PWA`
